GitHub

French Book Difficulty Analyzer
GitHub

I kept picking up French novels that destroyed me by chapter two with no way to know in advance. Upload your Anki deck and the difficulty score filters out words you already know — each gap shown with its real sentence from the book. Chunks fan out via RabbitMQ to spaCy workers, results stream live via WebSocket. A crashed worker gets its chunk redelivered, not silently dropped.

metrics
49ms avg per ~500-word chunk processed 1.8x speedup with 3 parallel spaCy workers 4,552 Anki lemmas cross-referenced per chunk for personalized scoring under 1s from processing to live difficulty curve via WebSocket
